SERVICE FAQS

Questions about
Internal Linking

Clear answers to common questions about the scope, process and practical considerations of this service.

Why is internal linking important for SEO?
Internal links help visitors discover related information and help search engines understand page relationships, site structure and the relative importance of URLs.
How do you decide which pages should link to each other?
I review topic relationships, page purpose, user journeys and site hierarchy. Links should connect genuinely related content and help the reader take a useful next step.
What makes a good internal link anchor text?
Useful anchor text describes the destination clearly and naturally in context. It should help the reader understand what they will find without repeating an exact-match phrase unnaturally across the site.
Can internal linking help important pages get discovered?
Yes. Relevant links from accessible pages can improve discovery and clarify relationships. They do not guarantee indexing or rankings, and should be supported by sound technical accessibility and useful page content.
