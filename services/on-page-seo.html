SERVICE FAQS

Questions about
On-Page SEO

Clear answers to common questions about the scope, process and practical considerations of this service.

What is included in an on-page SEO review?
An on-page review looks at search intent, page titles, meta descriptions, headings, content coverage, internal links, image context and how clearly each page communicates its subject and purpose.
How do you choose the right primary keyword for a page?
I assess the page's purpose, search intent, query language, business relevance and overlap with other URLs. The goal is to assign a clear topic to the page without forcing an unsuitable keyword into the content.
Do you rewrite website content as part of on-page SEO?
Content recommendations can include restructuring, clarifying sections, filling meaningful information gaps and improving headings or metadata. Rewriting is scoped to the page and should preserve accurate brand and service details.
How do you avoid keyword cannibalization?
I map the intent and topic of important URLs, identify pages competing for the same purpose and decide whether they need clearer differentiation, consolidation, redirects or stronger internal linking.
