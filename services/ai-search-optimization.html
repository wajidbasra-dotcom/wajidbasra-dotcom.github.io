SERVICE FAQS

Questions about
AI Search Optimization

Clear answers to common questions about the scope, process and practical considerations of this service.

What does AI search optimization involve?
AI search optimization focuses on making a website's information accessible, clear, well-structured and trustworthy for answer-led search experiences. It builds on sound technical SEO, useful content, entity clarity and supporting evidence.
Is AI search optimization different from traditional SEO?
It extends rather than replaces SEO. Crawlability, indexability, helpful content, clear page structure and credible information remain important, while the work also considers how answers may be assembled and sourced by AI-powered search experiences.
Can you guarantee that a brand will be cited in AI Overviews or AI answers?
No. AI search systems control which sources they use and how they present answers. Optimization can improve source clarity and accessibility, but no consultant can guarantee inclusion or citation.
How do you assess AI search visibility?
An assessment can review important topics and entities, source accessibility, content clarity, structured information and observed visibility across relevant AI search experiences. Findings should distinguish measured evidence from assumptions.
