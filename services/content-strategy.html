SERVICE FAQS

Questions about
Content Strategy

Clear answers to common questions about the scope, process and practical considerations of this service.

What does an SEO content strategy include?
A content strategy defines the audiences and search intents to serve, the topics and page types needed, how content supports business goals, and how pages should be structured, linked, maintained and measured.
How do you decide which content to create first?
Prioritization considers business value, search demand, intent, existing visibility, content gaps, topical overlap, resources and the ability to produce genuinely useful information.
Should a business create a separate page for every keyword?
No. Closely related queries with the same intent often belong on one strong page. Separate pages are justified when the searcher's needs, service scope or information requirements are meaningfully different.
Does content strategy include updating existing pages?
Yes. An effective strategy reviews existing URLs for accuracy, usefulness, overlap and missed opportunities. Updating, consolidating or retiring content can be more appropriate than continually publishing new pages.
