Selected Work

SEO work built around real search problems.

Representative project types across technical diagnostics, local search architecture, content systems and emerging AI search workflows.

Technical SEO01

Crawl governance, indexing & site architecture

Challenge: Duplicate URLs, inconsistent canonicals, deep pages and inefficient crawl paths.

Approach: Audit crawl behavior, sitemap hygiene, canonicals, internal links and page hierarchy; prioritize fixes by search impact.

Local SEO02

Multi-location architecture & local visibility

Challenge: Location pages that overlap, lack unique relevance or do not connect clearly to services.

Approach: Map service/location intent, strengthen local entity signals and create useful page relationships without thin duplication.

Content03

Topic architecture & content prioritization

Challenge: Large content inventories with unclear intent, weak topical relationships or outdated pages.

Approach: Cluster topics, map intent, prioritize high-value pages and create briefs around information gain and business relevance.

AI Search04

Entity clarity & generative search readiness

Challenge: Important brand, service and location information scattered across a site.

Approach: Improve entity consistency, supporting evidence, page structure, structured data and connected internal context.
