SERVICE FAQS

Questions about
Technical SEO

Clear answers to common questions about the scope, process and practical considerations of this service.

What does a technical SEO review cover?
A technical SEO review examines how search engines discover, crawl, render, interpret and index important pages. It can include crawl paths, indexation, canonicals, redirects, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, JavaScript rendering, site architecture and technical performance.
How do you prioritize technical SEO issues?
I prioritize issues by the pages and templates affected, their impact on search accessibility, the evidence available and the effort required to fix them. A clear priority list separates critical blockers from improvements that can be scheduled later.
Can technical SEO help pages that are not getting indexed?
Yes. A review can identify possible causes such as crawl barriers, canonical conflicts, weak internal linking, duplicate URLs, rendering problems or low-value page patterns. The right fix depends on what the URL-level evidence shows.
Do you implement technical SEO fixes or provide recommendations?
The scope can include diagnosis, implementation guidance, developer-ready recommendations and validation after changes. The exact level of implementation support is agreed around the website and team setup.
