About Wajid Basra

SEO strategy grounded in execution.

I am an SEO Manager and SEO & AI Search Consultant working across technical SEO, local search, content strategy, structured data and emerging search systems.

Professional Focus

From search fundamentals to AI discovery.

My work sits at the intersection of traditional search engineering and the newer systems shaping generative discovery. I work on the technical and content foundations that help websites become easier to crawl, understand and surface.

I have worked across international search markets and with agencies, businesses and marketing teams on technical SEO, local SEO, location-page strategy, content systems, Google Business Profile optimization, structured data and AI-assisted workflows.

The approach is practical: diagnose the search problem, prioritize what matters, implement clearly and build processes that teams can repeat.

Core areas

  • Local SEO and Google Business Profile strategy
  • Content architecture and search intent mapping
  • Schema.org and structured data
  • AI Search, GEO and LLM visibility
  • SEO automation and team training
Working Style

Clear diagnosis. Practical execution.
