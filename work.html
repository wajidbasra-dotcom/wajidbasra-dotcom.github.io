Representative examples of the search problems I work on, the decisions involved and the way I approach implementation.

TECHNICAL SEO01

Crawl governance, indexing & site architecture

Challenge

Duplicate or parameter URLs, inconsistent canonicals, deep pages and unclear crawl paths can make it harder to surface the URLs that matter.

Approach

Review crawl behavior, sitemap hygiene, canonicals, internal links, redirects and page hierarchy. Prioritize fixes by their effect on important URLs and validate implementation.

LOCAL SEO02

Multi-location architecture & local visibility

Challenge

Location pages can overlap, lack meaningful local information or fail to connect clearly to the services offered in each area.

Approach

Map service and location intent, plan city hubs and supporting service pages, strengthen local business information and create useful internal relationships.

CONTENT STRATEGY03

Topic architecture & content prioritization

Challenge

Large content inventories often contain overlapping pages, missing subtopics, outdated information or URLs with no clear search purpose.

Approach

Review the inventory, cluster topics, map intent, identify consolidation opportunities and prioritize new or refreshed pages against business relevance.

AI SEARCH04

Entity clarity & source readiness

Challenge

Important information about a business, its services and expertise may be scattered or described inconsistently across key pages.

Approach

Review source accessibility, entity descriptions, structured information, page clarity and supporting evidence. Assess relevant AI search surfaces without promising citations or placement.

These are representative project types, not named client case studies. Specific client details and performance figures are only published where approved and verified.
